## Why none can be published yet
ChargeSetu's database requires coordinates, connector types, power, number of charging points and opening hours for every station, and the site labels a station "verified" only after the team confirms those details. No source found for any lead provides all of them for a charger that is confirmed public and working today, and publishing guesses would put wrong information in front of drivers.

## Limits of this research
- Operator apps and locators (Jio-bp, Tata Power EZ Charge, Bolt.Earth, GoGreen, GreenShift) cannot be read by automated tools, so station-level operator confirmation was not possible for those networks.
- Tesla and PlugShare pages blocked automated access; only their search-result text was seen.
- Google Maps was not accessible. OpenStreetMap has only 9 charging points in the Mumbai / Navi Mumbai area searched and none lists connectors or hours.
- NITI Aayog's E-Amrit station database was not used because its owner asks for prior permission before use.
- Ather's PDF and the MSEDCL article are undated; neither proves a charger works today.
