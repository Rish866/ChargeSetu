## Full description — English
ChargeSetu helps electric car, scooter and auto drivers find charging stations across India.

• Search by state, city or area, or find stations near you
• Filter by vehicle (car or 2W/3W), AC or DC, connector (CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO, Bharat AC/DC…), power and operator
• Nationwide map with clustered markers
• One tap for directions in Google Maps
• Clear labels: "Verified" when the operator or our team confirmed the details; "Source-listed · status unconfirmed" when the location comes from open map data and has not been checked
• Unknown details are shown as "not provided", never guessed
• Report wrong information, and charge point operators can submit or claim their stations
• English and हिन्दी

What ChargeSetu does not do: it does not show live charger availability, take bookings, or process payments. Always check with the operator before a long trip.

Station data includes © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
