# Play Console — Data safety answers (based on the current code)

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share user data? | **Yes, collects; does not share** | Accounts and reports are stored in ChargeSetu's database (Supabase). No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising. |
| Is data encrypted in transit? | **Yes** | HTTPS only (cleartext blocked in the app). |
| Can users request deletion? | **Yes** | Via the contact/support email (see Privacy Policy). |
| Location | **Approximate + precise, optional, not stored** | Used once in the browser to sort stations by distance; the coordinates are put in the page address, not saved to an account. |
| Personal info: email, name | **Collected (optional)** for operator/admin accounts and optional email on issue reports | Account management, follow-up on reports. |
| App activity, device IDs, contacts, photos, files, messages, health, financial | **Not collected** | No analytics or advertising SDKs are included. |

Content rating: utility app. There is no user-generated public content: reports are private, and operator submissions are moderated before they are published.
Ads: **No ads.**
Target audience: 18+ (drivers and operators).
